# T&C Guardian dynamic logo set

Created using built-in image generation. Three standalone transparent PNG designs with a shared shield shape:

- `guardian-high-risk.png`: angry red guardian and steam puff.
- `guardian-medium-risk.png`: worried amber guardian and a small flame.
- `guardian-low-risk.png`: happy green heart-shaped tree inside the guardian shield.

Intended behavior: select the icon for the highest detected risk for each website. Low-risk and zero-findings results can share the green icon while the popup states which result applies. These are image assets; automated switching is not implemented in this deliverable. The source files are high-resolution PNGs, not pixel-tuned toolbar-size exports.

## Generation prompts

### High risk

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: the HIGH-RISK state of a dynamic browser extension logo for "T&C Guardian". Create ONE standalone textless app icon on a truly transparent background, square 1024x1024 composition. A very cute but ANGRY red guardian SHIELD mascot, simple bold flat vector-like illustration, crisp clean contours, strong small-size readability. A compact rounded shield with gently curved shoulders and a rounded point, taking up about 78% of the canvas width and height with generous transparent padding. Bold continuous dark burgundy outline, warm strawberry-red fill with a small restrained coral highlight. Inside: large expressive dark burgundy slanted angry eyebrows and simple round dark eyes, small cute determined frown, two simple rosy cheeks. It must read as a protective little guardian who is angry about bad privacy policies, adorable rather than scary. A tiny stylized red steam puff next to its upper right edge to reinforce anger, but keep the silhouette compact. No arms or legs, no lettering, no brand text, no scene, no container tile, no gradient background, no watermark, no drop shadow cast onto a background. Minimal details and sturdy thick strokes appropriate for a browser toolbar icon. This red shield will later have coordinated amber small-flame and green heart-tree variants, so keep the base shield silhouette clean and reusable. Genuine alpha transparency, not a painted checkerboard.

### Medium risk

Reference image: guardian-high-risk.png

Use case: logo-brand. Edit the supplied red guardian shield logo to create its matching MEDIUM-RISK AMBER variant. Preserve exactly the same rounded shield silhouette, thick outline weight, overall composition and approximate scale, cute visual style, circular eyes, blush cheeks and restrained highlight. Truly transparent background. Change red fill to warm golden yellow with dark warm brown outline and facial features. Change angry brows and frown to a cute worried or apprehensive expression, raised uneven brows and a tiny round mouth: the guardian is getting warm and almost about to catch fire. Replace the red steam puff at the upper-right with ONE SMALL stylized orange flame with a golden inner core, just licking the shield's upper-right shoulder, restrained and compact, not engulfing it. The amber medium-risk state must look less intense than the angry red high-risk state. One simple textless icon, no arms, no legs, no scenery, no letters, no container tile, no background or ground shadow. Bold clean vector-like forms, browser extension logo family, genuine alpha transparency, square canvas same as supplied. Keep generous padding and do not crop the flame.

### Low risk / clean

Reference image: guardian-high-risk.png

Use case: logo-brand. Edit the supplied red guardian shield logo to create its matching GREEN LOW-RISK/CLEAN variant with a HEART-SHAPED TREE. Preserve the same rounded shield outer silhouette, thick outline weight, overall scale, bold clean vector-like forms and cute brand personality. Truly transparent background. Recolor shield outer outline deep forest green, shield interior very pale mint. Remove the angry face and red steam puff completely. INSIDE THE SHIELD put ONE bold simple tree whose foliage canopy is unmistakably a SINGLE LARGE HEART SHAPE, fresh rich leaf green, with a deep green outline, a short centered warm-brown trunk below that forks into two simple branches. Put the cute happy guardian FACE inside the HEART-SHAPED TREE CANOPY: two simple curved smiling eyes, a little smile and soft rosy blush cheeks. The heart-tree face should be large and readable, occupying most of the shield interior, with the small trunk still clearly visible under the heart, so it reads as a heart-shaped TREE protected by a shield, not just a floating heart. A tiny leaf at upper-right may replace the original steam puff, but no clutter. One standalone centered textless app icon, no words, no letter, no arms or legs, no landscape, no container tile, no shadows on a background. Keep details minimal for browser toolbar use. Genuine alpha transparency, same square canvas as reference.
